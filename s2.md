# Section 2 - Core Gameplay

## 2.1 Gameplay Overview

The player controls a small combat robot inside a single arena viewed from above.

The objective is to survive for as long as possible while progressively becoming stronger through temporary equipment and permanently expanding the available gameplay configurations.

The arena has no predefined objective beyond survival. Enemy pressure continuously increases until the player dies or, in future versions, defeats a boss encounter.

---

# 2.2 Match Flow

Every match follows the same high-level structure.

```
Start Run
      │
Spawn Player
      │
Fight Enemies
      │
Collect XP
      │
Level Up
      │
Unlock Configuration
      │
Activate Configurations (optional)
      │
Collect Weapons / Equipment
      │
Enemies Become Stronger
      │
Repeat
      │
Player Dies
      │
End Run
```

Only permanent configurations survive between runs.

---

# 2.3 Player

The player controls a small combat robot.

The robot has no dialogue, story or personality that affects gameplay.

Its purpose is to serve as the player's avatar.

The design should communicate:

* simplicity
* machinery
* durability
* slightly comedic appearance

---

# 2.4 Camera

Camera perspective:

* Top-down
* Camera follows the player
* Small smoothing/lag effect instead of snapping directly to the player's position

Purpose:

* movement feels less rigid
* gives slightly more dynamic motion
* avoids making the camera feel glued to the player

---

# 2.5 Movement

Two movement modes exist.

## Standard Movement

Movement uses configurable keybinds.

Default:

* W
* A
* S
* D

Movement is omnidirectional.

Diagonal movement is allowed.

---

## Mouse Movement Mode

Unlocked later as a gameplay configuration.

When enabled:

* the robot continuously walks toward the current mouse cursor
* keyboard movement becomes disabled
* movement never stops unless the cursor is directly over the player

Reward:

* +40% multiplicative drop chance

Flavor Text:

> **Autopilot**
>
> *"Thinking is expensive."*

---

# 2.6 Health

Base HP:

150

Maximum HP may increase through gameplay configurations.

HP cannot naturally regenerate.

Healing only comes from:

* healing drops
* temporary equipment
* future items

---

## Death

The player dies immediately when HP reaches zero.

Current run ends.

Temporary progress is lost.

Permanent configuration unlocks remain saved.

---

# 2.7 Damage

Enemies damage the player by contact.

Each enemy maintains its own damage cooldown.

Example:

Enemy A touches player.

400 ms cooldown begins.

Enemy B touches player.

Enemy B uses its own cooldown independently.

This value is not global.

Each enemy archetype defines:

* damage
* cooldown
* movement speed

Example:

Fast enemy

Damage:
8

Cooldown:
250 ms

Tank

Damage:
20

Cooldown:
700 ms

This makes enemies feel mechanically different without requiring complicated AI.

---

# 2.8 Combat

Combat is intentionally straightforward.

The player has two permanent weapons.

Additional temporary weapons may be collected during the run.

---

# 2.9 Starting Weapons

## Melee Attack

Characteristics:

* short range
* low damage
* attacks in an area
* pushes back up to two nearest enemies

Purpose:

* emergency crowd control
* escape surrounded situations

Knockback should be noticeable but not excessive.

Flavor Text:

> **Maintenance Wrench**
>
> *"Percussive maintenance fixes most things."*

---

## Pistol

Characteristics:

* infinite ammunition
* low damage
* slow projectile speed
* single target
* long range

Purpose:

Reliable default weapon.

Never disappears.

Flavor Text:

> **Factory Standard**
>
> *"It technically qualifies as 'armed'."*

---

# 2.10 Weapon Slots

Player inventory contains four slots.

Slot 1

Permanent melee weapon.

Cannot be replaced.

---

Slot 2

Permanent pistol.

Cannot be replaced.

---

Slot 3

Temporary special weapon.

Can hold exactly one weapon.

Picking another replaces the current one.

---

Slot 4

Temporary equipment.

Reserved for support gadgets.

Only one at a time.

---

# 2.11 Special Weapons

Special weapons are obtained from weapon drops.

They become active immediately after pickup.

The player uses the same fire button regardless of weapon.

Weapons with automatic fire continuously shoot while the fire button is held.

Weapons requiring repeated shots must be clicked repeatedly.

Current planned weapons include:

## Minigun

High fire rate.

High DPS.

Large spread.

Limited ammunition.

---

## Shotgun

Very high burst damage.

Wide pellet spread.

Short effective range.

---

## Assault Rifle

Automatic.

Balanced damage.

Medium spread.

General-purpose weapon.

---

## Rocket Launcher

Explosive damage.

Area of effect.

Very limited ammunition.

Slow projectiles.

---

## Grenade Launcher

Explosive damage.

More ammunition than rocket launcher.

Projectile arc may be introduced in future versions.

---

Additional weapons may be introduced later following the same philosophy.

---

# 2.12 Ammunition

Special weapons use limited ammunition.

Once ammunition reaches zero:

* weapon disappears
* Slot 3 becomes empty

Permanent weapons never consume ammunition.

---

# 2.13 Equipment

Equipment occupies Slot 4.

Equipment is temporary.

Duration:

30 seconds.

Picking another equipment immediately replaces the current one.

Current examples:

Healing Drone

Periodically heals player.

---

Combat Drone

Automatically attacks nearby enemies.

---

Support Drone

Provides temporary buffs.

---

Equipment is obtained exclusively through drops.

---

# 2.14 Experience

Enemies grant XP when defeated.

Different enemy archetypes grant different amounts.

Collected XP fills the player's experience bar.

Experience is represented using a yellow bar beneath the player.

---

# 2.15 Level Up

When enough XP is collected:

Game pauses.

Level-up menu opens.

Player receives three random locked configurations.

If fewer than three locked configurations remain:

Show only the remaining ones.

Player selects exactly one.

The selected configuration becomes permanently unlocked.

The game then resumes.

Configurations never appear again once unlocked.

---

# 2.16 Maximum Level

There is no hardcoded maximum level.

Instead:

Maximum level is determined by the number of remaining locked gameplay configurations.

Once every gameplay configuration has been unlocked:

Further level-ups become impossible.

The implementation should calculate this automatically.

This allows future configurations to increase the maximum level without modifying gameplay code.

---

# 2.17 Run Progression

Enemy intensity increases continuously.

Scaling includes:

* larger waves
* more enemies
* stronger archetypes

The objective is to create increasing pressure instead of discrete difficulty spikes.

---

# 2.18 Victory

Current version has no mandatory victory condition.

The intended gameplay loop is survival until death.

Future versions may introduce:

* boss encounters
* survival milestones
* optional completion objectives

These additions should not fundamentally alter the survival-focused gameplay loop.

---

# 2.19 Core Design Principles

The gameplay should remain readable at all times.

Complexity comes from:

* choosing configurations
* deciding when to replace weapons
* adapting to enemy archetypes

Complexity should **not** come from:

* difficult controls
* complicated weapon combos
* intricate enemy AI

The player's attention should stay focused on movement, positioning, resource collection and configuration choices.

Simple mechanics interacting with each other should create the challenge, rather than individually complex systems.
